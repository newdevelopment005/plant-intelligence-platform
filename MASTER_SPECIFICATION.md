# Plant Intelligence Platform (PIP)

## Vision

The Plant Intelligence Platform (PIP) is an enterprise-grade AI-powered scientific research platform for plant science.

The platform will integrate:

- Plant phenotyping
- Plant genetics
- Plant genomics
- Molecular biology
- Bioinformatics
- Scientific literature
- Knowledge graphs
- Laboratory Information Management
- Electronic Laboratory Notebook
- AI research assistant

The platform is intended for:

- Universities
- Agricultural research institutes
- Seed companies
- Government laboratories
- International research centres

The system must be scalable, modular, secure, maintainable and production-ready.

---

# Core Principles
