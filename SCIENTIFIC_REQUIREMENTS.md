# Plant Intelligence Platform (PIP)

## Scientific Requirements Specification

Version: 1.0

---

# Vision

The Plant Intelligence Platform (PIP) is an AI-powered scientific ecosystem designed to support research, breeding, genomics, molecular biology, bioinformatics, and precision agriculture.

The system shall provide researchers with an integrated platform capable of storing, analysing, interpreting and visualising scientific data across the complete plant research lifecycle.

The AI shall behave as an experienced multidisciplinary plant scientist while remaining evidence-based and transparent about uncertainty.

# AI Behaviour

The AI must

Never fabricate references.

Always provide citations.

Explain uncertainty.

Differentiate evidence from opinion.

Use current scientific terminology.

Recommend validation experiments.

Never present speculative conclusions as established facts.

Prefer peer-reviewed evidence.

Respect uploaded institutional data.

Maintain scientific neutrality.
